I'm Fran, a remote professional with 15 years of experience and a background in digital marketing implementation, CRM workflows, funnel building, email marketing, website management and business operations.

Across different client projects, I've supported the implementation and maintenance of the systems that help businesses attract leads, assess prospects, personalise what people receive, nurture relationships, book calls, promote events and support sales.

What I bring is an understanding of how the pieces connect. I think about what happens when a lead enters a funnel, where their information is stored, which tags and fields make personalisation possible, which workflow they should enter, and what should happen if they book, don't show up or buy.

I work comfortably on the customer-facing side, the pages and emails people see, and on the backend that makes it behave correctly. When something breaks, I trace it step by step until I find where the journey and the system stopped matching.
